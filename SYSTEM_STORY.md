## 1. System Overview
Our campus is deploying a new **"Smart Parking, LPR, and EV (Electric Vehicle) Network"** to manage parking congestion, automated gate access, EV charging billing, and enforcement across campus parking lots.

---

## 2. Core Functional Modules & Business Rules

### 2.1 Access Control and Automated Gate Entry (LPR & Guest QR)
* The parking gates are equipped with **License Plate Recognition (LPR)** cameras interfaced via `LprGateBoundary`.
* When a vehicle approaches an entrance gate, the external **LPR System** scans the license plate and notifies `ReservationControl`.
* If the vehicle belongs to a registered **Student** or **Faculty** member with an **active parking permit**, the barrier gate opens automatically.
* Unregistered **Guest** vehicles are denied entry unless they scan a valid temporary **QR code** (`GuestPass`) generated by the **Campus Security Office** (`SecurityGuard`).

### 2.2 Dynamic Spot Discovery and 15-Minute Reservations
* Registered users (**Students** and **Faculty**) search for available spots via `StudentDashboardUI`.
* **Dynamic Zone Filtering:** Students can only view and reserve spots in the **"Student Zone"**, whereas Faculty members can view and reserve spots across **all zones** (`ParkingZone`).
* A user can reserve either a **Standard Spot** or an **EV Charging Spot** (`ParkingSpot`) for a maximum window of **15 minutes** (`Reservation`).
* **No-Show Enforcement:** If the LPR camera does not detect the reserved vehicle entering within **15 minutes** of reservation creation, `ReservationControl` automatically revokes the reservation (`NO_SHOW_REVOKED`), terminates the active reservation instance, and increments the user's `noShowWarnings` counter.

### 2.3 Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging and Idle Penalties
* To use an **EV Charging Spot**, `EvBillingControl` first processes a **credit card payment authorization** (`PaymentAuthorization`) via the external Payment Gateway (`PaymentGatewayBoundary`).
* Once vehicle battery charging reaches **100% completion**, `EvBillingControl` sends an automated **SMS notification** via the external SMS Gateway (`SmsGatewayBoundary`).
* **Charger Idle Penalty:** If the user does not unplug and physically vacate the EV bay within **30 minutes** of the 100% completion notification, `EvBillingControl` automatically creates a **"Charger Idle Penalty" of 5 NIS per hour** (`Violation`) until the spot is physically vacated.

### 2.4 Security Enforcement and Digital Appeals
* **Campus Security Guards** (`SecurityGuard`) patrol the lots using a mobile terminal (`GuardTerminalUI`).
* When a guard spots an illegally parked vehicle (e.g., parked over two lines or occupying a disabled spot without a permit), they log a **Severe Violation** (`Violation`) via `EnforcementControl`, attached to the vehicle's license plate.
* **Fine Attachment & Parallel Notification:** When a violation is logged, `EnforcementControl` simultaneously attaches the fine to the vehicle owner's account (blocking annual permit renewal until resolved) AND dispatches a violation notification to the user.
* **Digital Appeal Workflow:** Users can submit a **Digital Appeal** (`Appeal`) through `StudentDashboardUI`. Every appeal is reviewed and either **Approved** (canceling the fine) or **Rejected** (enforcing the fine) by the **Campus Traffic Administrator** (`TrafficAdministrator`) via `AdminAppealUI`.

## 3. Edge Cases & Concurrency Rules (Approved in Exercise 1)
1. **Simultaneous Spot Reservation (Race Condition):** Two users attempting to reserve the last available spot at the exact same millisecond must be serialized using a version counter or atomic reservation check (`version` attribute and `tryReserve(expectedVersion)` on `ParkingSpot`) so only one reservation succeeds.
2. **Physical Bay Occupancy vs. Electrical Cable Disconnection:** Unplugging the EV cable at minute 29 without physically moving the car out of the EV bay must still trigger the 30-minute Charger Idle Penalty (`isPluggedIn` vs. `isPhysicallyOccupied` on `ChargingSession`).
